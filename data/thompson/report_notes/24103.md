# 24103 — Fish Passage Culvert Inspection (FPCI): Various Sites in the Adams Lake Watershed within the Kamloops and Headwaters Forest Districts, FIA Project Number 5041001
- **Author / organisation:** Michele Trumbley, RPBio and Nicole Plysiuk, BNRSc; Trumbley Environmental Consulting Ltd. (TEC). Prepared for International Forest Products Ltd. (Interfor), Adams Lake Lumber Division.   **Year:** 2008 (submitted Nov 28, 2008)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** Forest Investment Account, project 5041001 (p. 4)
- **Region check:** elsewhere — South Thompson. All 17 sites are in the Adams Lake watershed (Adams River / Shuswap Lake system), Kamloops and Headwaters Forest Districts, not the North Thompson. Streams named: Gollen Creek, Spapillem Creek and tributary, tributaries to Tsikwustum Lake, Eberts (Athelstan) Creek, Pass Creek, Brennan Creek and tributary, Fisher Creek, Gannet Creek; roads: Adams West FSR (34.5, 41, 57.5 km), road 570-300 (pp. 12–18).
- **Type:** fish passage assessment (Phase 1 culvert inspection under the MOE 2008 protocol)

## What was done
Interfor pre-selected 17 culvert crossings from local knowledge plus FSID, FHAP and FFHI data; the site list was approved by Mark Phillpotts, MOE Kamloops, on Aug 11, 2008 (p. 6). Field method, scoring (Table 2 p. 8), habitat rating and analysis rules are the same as in TEC's TFL 18 report (24097). Cost-benefit and an Interfor-ranked implementation list were produced (pp. 23–25). Field dates are not stated beyond 2008.

## Findings
- 6 high-risk full barriers with confirmed fish (Table 3 p. 12): AL-11 Gollen Creek (score 39, 3300 mm CMP, HGI 9,909 m, fish observed upstream by Silvatech 2001), AL-6 trib to Spapillem Creek (36), AL-15 trib to Tsikwustum Lake (31), AL-17 trib to Tsikwustum Lake (29), AL-3 Eberts Creek (29), AL-7 Spapillem Creek (21).
- 0 high-risk barriers without confirmed fish; 1 moderate: AL-5 Spapillem Creek wooden box culvert, scored passable (13) but recommended for replacement because support logs obstruct passage (p. 14). 2 low risk / passable with fish confirmed: AL-8 Pass Creek (10), AL-16 vehicle ford on trib to Tsikwustum Lake, which obstructs passage though fish were seen both sides (p. 16). 8 non-priority sites, mostly barriers with natural falls or cascades close upstream/downstream (AL-1, AL-2, AL-4, AL-9, AL-10) or open-bottom log bridges on Gannet Creek to monitor (AL-12, -13, -14) (pp. 17–18).
- 11 sites met the FIA minimum score of 20; 19.3 km of habitat potentially gained (p. 2).

## Recommendations and priorities
Interfor priority ranking for replacement (Table 9 p. 25): 1 AL-3 Eberts Creek (bridge $74K), 2 AL-5 (bridge $89K), 3 AL-7 (remove structure $7K), 4 AL-15 (arch $29K), 5 AL-17 (arch $52K), 6 AL-11 Gollen Creek (bridge $115K), 7 AL-6 (arch $58K). A second field visit may be needed to verify TRIM-derived habitat gain.

## Useful for crossing work today
Not relevant to North Thompson crossing planning: every site is in the Adams Lake watershed (South Thompson). It is a sibling of 24097 using identical methods, and its 2008-protocol data are likely in PSCIS for the Adams Lake area.
