# 24436 — Fish Passage Culvert Inspection (FPCI): Tributaries to the Raft River, Hole in the Wall FSR
- **Author / organisation:** Doug Wahl, RPBio, CPESC, Snowy River Resources Ltd. (Summerland BC); field crew Doug Wahl and Jenine Mylymok. Prepared for Canadian Forest Products Ltd., Vavenby Division (FIA coordinator Dave Dobi, RPF).   **Year:** 2006 (field September 7–9 and October 5–6, 2006; report submitted December 10, 2006)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** Forest Investment Account (FIA); EcoCat title carries FIA# 4596003 (p. 1).
- **Region check:** in the North Thompson (LNTH) — Raft River sub-basin, east side of the Raft River along the Hole in the Wall (HITW) FSR, northeast of Clearwater, near Silence Lake (Figures 1–2, p. 7). Sites are on 1:20,000 mapsheet 082M.072, Headwaters Forest District (p. 35). Covers 26 crossings on unnamed tributaries to the Raft River plus Blowhole Creek.
- **Type:** fish passage assessment (FPCI, Parker 2000 procedure — equivalent to a PSCIS Phase 1 culvert inspection with FPCI scoring), with fish presence/absence and upper-limit-of-fish surveys.

## What was done
Pre-field selection of culverts on known/suspected/default fish-streams along the HITW FSR, dropping reaches >20% gradient (25% on TRIM) (p. 3). No prior fisheries inventory existed for these tributaries; the 1997 ARC Environmental / North Thompson Indian Band Raft River FHAP covered only the mainstem (p. 3). Field crew walked channels upstream (and downstream) of each crossing to find the upper limit of fish, measured culverts (Global Flow Probe velocity, clinometer slope, outfall drop, pool depth) and rated habitat 200–300 m upstream (pp. 3–4). Sites scored with the FPCI matrix (max 55; FIA threshold 39) (p. 5). 25 culverts and 1 vehicle ford assessed, n = 26 sites (p. 6). Full FPCI forms completed only at Sites 13, 15 and 26 (pp. 35–39).
Streams named: Tributaries 1–25 (plus 12A, 12B, 17A, 19A) to the Raft River, all unnamed; Blowhole Creek (Site 26). Road: Hole in the Wall FSR; Rd. 40 near Sites 24–26. Site UTMs (Zone 11) are given on each site page, e.g. Site 13 314207E 5738456N; Site 15 314410E 5737923N; Site 16 314428E 5737312N; Site 17 314433E 5737129N; Site 19 313933E 5736092N; Site 26 313870E 5731906N. No PSCIS ids.

## Findings
Summary (p. 6): 6 fish-streams at the FSR, 1 full barrier, 5 partial barriers, 20 non fish-streams, 1 vehicle ford (Site 5, non fish-stream). Twenty tributaries (Sites 1–12, 14, 17A, 18, 20–25) were certified non fish-streams at the crossing, mostly because gradients of 23–47% or channel dissipation lie 50–110 m below the road (Table 3 pp. 9–10; site pages pp. 11–32).
- **Site 13** (600 mm CMP, 6 m, S3): full barrier, 0.68 m outlet drop, FPCI 42 — the only site meeting the FIA threshold. Rainbow trout captured by electrofishing ~350 m upstream of the FSR and observed downstream; upstream habitat rated good rearing / moderate spawning; 1,740 m of habitat above the barrier, 82% of stream barred; assessment terminated 575 m upstream with fish assumed further up (pp. 22, 35–36).
- **Site 15** (1200 mm CMP, 8 m, 7% slope, dry at survey, 0.22 m drop, 242% undersized for Q100): partial barrier, FPCI 38; rainbow trout plus unidentified salmonid observed ~300 m upstream; permanent bedrock falls (1.5, 2.5, 3.0 m) 575 m upstream are the upper limit of fish; upstream rearing and spawning rated good; 575 m gained, 66% barred (pp. 24, 37–38).
- **Site 16** (600 mm CMP): partial barrier, 50% filled with bedload, too short; rainbow trout 300 m downstream of the FSR; boulder cascade (25% over 50 m) upstream limits habitat; not scored (p. 25).
- **Site 17**: partial barrier, channel dissipates ~50 m upstream; not scored (p. 26).
- **Site 19** (600 mm CMP): partial barrier, 24 cm outlet drop; rainbow trout observed 200 m upstream of the FSR; reach break (35% falls/cascade) 408 m upstream; not scored (p. 29).
- **Site 26, Blowhole Creek** (2 × 600 mm + 1 × 400 mm CMP, lower crossing): partial barrier by velocity and 8–30 cm drops, 330% undersized for Q100, FPCI 31; rainbow trout plus unidentified salmonid; Reach 2 above the upper crossing is a confirmed non fish-stream (p. 33).
- Fish species: rainbow trout only (plus unidentified salmonids); no other species named.

## Recommendations and priorities
- Site 13: replace culvert with a low-profile bridge or arch; channel reconstruction and an environmental monitor needed; CanFor had a site plan in preparation (pp. 22, 36).
- Site 15: replace with a low-profile bridge; FIA approved a variance for this site (score 38) after a Forsite submission in October 2006; site plan in preparation (pp. 6, 24).
- Site 26: FIA declined the variance (score 31); authors still recommend removing the culverts and installing an open-bottom structure (pp. 6, 33).
- Site 16: replace with an arch or low-profile bridge (capacity and fill stability, not only passage) (p. 25).
- Site 19: either maintain the narrow road width if the short culvert stays, or replace with an open-bottom structure (p. 29).
- Site 17: do not replace with a fish-passable structure (very limited habitat); armour inlet/outlet and overflow pipe (p. 26).
- Sites 12A and 17A: install a new cross-drain where the braid first meets the FSR ditch (pp. 21, 27). Site 5: install a culvert at the ford, fish passage not required (p. 15).
- No cost figures given.

## Useful for crossing work today
For the Hole in the Wall FSR this report settles which crossings matter: Sites 13 and 15 carry resident rainbow trout above the road and were slated for bridge replacement in 2006–07 (check whether the works were completed before re-prioritising them), Blowhole Creek (Site 26) is a partial barrier that was never funded, and 20 crossings are certified non fish-streams with upper-limit-of-fish coordinates that can pre-empt Phase 1 field visits. The site UTMs make it possible to match to PSCIS, but this is an FIA-era FPCI, not a PSCIS submission, so the barrier results and the fish observations are unlikely to be in PSCIS or FISS unless entered later.
