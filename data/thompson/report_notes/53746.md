# 53746 — Callazon/Clearwater Fish Habitat (letter report, Fletcher Challenge Canada Limited)
- **Author / organisation:** David J. Lindsay, R.P.Bio., Fish and Wildlife Biologist, Integrated Resource Analysis Section, Fletcher Challenge Canada Limited (Crofton, BC); addressed to Glen Dick, Fletcher Challenge Canada Ltd., Mackenzie Region   **Year:** 1993 (field visit October 8, 1993; letter dated October 13, 1993)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** industry (Fletcher Challenge Canada Ltd.), with technical guidance from Ministry of Environment (Fort St. John and Mackenzie); not FIA/FRBC
- **Region check:** **elsewhere — not the North Thompson.** The "Clearwater" here is the Callazon/Clearwater watershed reached from Pine Pass in the Mackenzie / Fort St. John area of northeastern BC (cc list is MoE Fort St. John, MoE Mackenzie, MoF Mackenzie, Dawson Creek and Prince George; the letter is stamped received by MoE Fort St. John). It is a different Clearwater River from the North Thompson tributary and was presumably harvested on the name match. Streams named are Callazon Creek tributaries (Doonan, Brandner, Ashe creeks) and Clearwater River tributaries (Clearwater Creek, Big Hill Creek, McCoun Creek, Cold Creek).
- **Type:** other — pre-construction fish habitat / fish passage requirement assessment for proposed forest road crossings on the Clearwater/Callazon FSR

## What was done
One-day field visit (October 8, 1993) along the Clearwater/Callazon F.S.R. to assess fish habitat at proposed crossing sites and decide whether fish passage was required for the drainage structures. Stream survey cards (DFO/MOEP forms) were filled only at the "more contentious" sites; photos and cards attached. About 22 sites were visited, referenced by road km and station: Callazon tributary crossings at 8.0 km (Doonan Creek), 9.1, 10.5 (Brandner Creek), 12 (Ashe Creek), 14.3, 16.0 km; Clearwater River crossings at 17.3, 20.7, 23, 26.1 km; lower Clearwater sites at 29, 36 km, Clearwater Creek (Station 40+100), 42 km (Big Hill Creek), 46 km (McCoun Creek), 47, 50, 50.5, 51, 53.5 and 59 km (Cold Creek). Cold water (3.5–5.0 °C) precluded fish sampling.

## Findings
Per-site professional judgement rather than sampling. Fish passage judged required (bridge or open-bottom/box culvert proposed) at Doonan Creek, Brandner Creek, Ashe Creek, the 17.3 km tributary to Clearwater Lake, 29 km, 36 km, Clearwater Creek, Big Hill Creek, McCoun Creek, 47 km (culvert with baffles possible; fish presence unknown, sampling recommended) and 51 km. Passage judged not required at high-gradient (10–15 %), <1–2 m wide, or dry/underground-flow sites (9.1, 14.3, 16.0, 20.7, 23, 26.1, 50, 50.5, 53.5 km) and at Cold Creek (59 km), where three sets of bedrock falls ~300 m above the mainstem preclude fish access. Natural barriers (debris jams, bedrock) noted at 10.5 km (temporary), 20.7 km and 23 km. No fish captured or observed.

## Recommendations and priorities
Structure-by-structure: bridges, natural-bottom or concrete box culverts where passage is required; corrugated steel pipes where not. Sediment control per Fletcher Challenge "Standard Operating Procedures – For Works in and About a Stream" (Sept 14, 1993). Fish sampling recommended at the 47 km crossing before construction. Most construction scheduled for summer 1994 after "a more definitive determination of fish presence". No costs given.

## Useful for crossing work today
Nothing for North Thompson planning: the report is about a forest road in the Peace/Mackenzie region. It should be flagged as a false match for the LNTH/UNTH corpus (name collision on "Clearwater") and excluded from the North Thompson priority farm. Not PSCIS-related.
