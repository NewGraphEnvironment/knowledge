- **Author / organisation:** Michael Foster, P.Eng, Forsite Consultants Ltd., Salmon Arm. Recipient: Marino Bordin, International Forest Products (Interfor), Adams Lake Lumber Division; Headwaters District, Kamloops Region (p. 1).   **Year:** fiscal 2006 (construction March 5–20, 2007; original culvert assessment June 9, 2006) (p. 1)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** Forest Investment Account — Investment Schedule SOTSA114598, Project 4598014 (p. 1).
- **Region check:** drainage not stated. The crossing is "in the George Creek Watershed (within the Burton Creek Watershed)" at UTM Zone 11 328384E 5708839N (≈51.50 N, 119.47 W), 34.3 km southeast along the Vavenby/Adams FSR from Vavenby (p. 1). That point lies between the North Thompson at Vavenby and the upper Adams River valley, so it may drain to the Adams (South Thompson system) rather than the North Thompson; confirm against FWA before counting it as LNTH. No crossing within 300 m of this UTM in the local LNTH/UNTH PSCIS extracts.
- **Type:** remediation (bridge replacing barrier culverts), with a summary of the preceding Phase 1 assessment.

## What was done
Snowy River Resources Ltd. assessed the George Creek crossing for Interfor on June 9, 2006: two 1200 mm CMPs, one a full barrier and one a partial barrier, FPCI score 40, crossing age estimated at 15+ years with bedload accumulating above the inlets; rainbow trout observed in the stream; S3 stream, not in a community watershed (p. 1). Forsite designed an 11 m concrete-slab bridge on integrated cap beams with wingwalls on spread footings, sloped at 4.5% to match the mainline grade (p. 1). Excavation began March 5, 2007 and the bridge was complete March 20, 2007; cap beams were installed on the wrong ends on March 8, caught at the March 13 inspection, and the bridge was dismantled and re-set on March 20 (p. 1). Stream: George Creek. Road: Vavenby/Adams FSR at 34.3 km. No PSCIS id.

## Findings
Pre-project: one full and one partial barrier culvert on a rainbow trout stream, FPCI 40 (p. 1). Post-project: fish passage restored by an open-span bridge; no post-construction fish data. Nothing on fish presence upstream beyond "rainbow trout were observed in the stream".

## Recommendations and priorities
A post-construction inspection of the approach fills is recommended, because they were built in wet conditions and may compact under hauling (p. 1). Cost summary (p. 1): survey and general arrangement $3,500.00; engineering supervision and reporting $4,190.66; L100 bridge structure and delivery $39,535.19; installation $21,400.00; project administration $6,315.84; materials $514.85; total $75,456.54.

## Useful for crossing work today
The George Creek crossing on the Vavenby/Adams FSR (34.3 km) has been an 11 m bridge since March 2007, so any older culvert record there is obsolete and the site should not be re-listed as a barrier. The $75k all-in cost for a 2007 L100 slab bridge is a useful historical benchmark. Whether the site belongs to the North Thompson or the Adams drainage needs a map check; the record is probably not in PSCIS (FIA-era, no PSCIS id, no match in the local extracts).
