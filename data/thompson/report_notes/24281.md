# 24281 — Fish Stream Identification, Various Tributaries: S258-Unnamed tributary to North Thompson River; M202-Outlet of Cindy Lake, Inlet of Bob Lake; M167-Edfee Lake Inlet; I141-Unnamed tributary to Maury Lake
- **Author / organisation:** Michele Trumbley, RPBio and Nicole Plysiuk, BIT; Trumbley Environmental Consulting Ltd. Prepared for Kris Cooke, RPF, Canadian Forest Products Ltd., Vavenby Operations.   **Year:** 2011 (submitted Nov 25, 2011; field Sept 12–14, 2011 under Scientific Fish Collection Permit KA11-73175)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** not stated (operational stream classification for Canfor cutting permits I141, M202, M167, S258)
- **Region check:** in the North Thompson (LNTH) and Mahood. Four Canfor Vavenby blocks on TFL 18: an unnamed direct tributary to the North Thompson River (WSC 129-535800) at 18 km on the Caribou Mountain FSR (UTM zone 11, ~328900/5722400; block S258); Cindy Lake outlet (WSC 129-360400-17900-77200-6419, Mann/Swayback FSR Road 6 at 31 km, TFL 18) and Bob Lake inlet (WBID 0005LNTH, Road 6 at 20 km) in block M202 — the Mann Creek system, LNTH; Efdee Lake inlet S4#2 (WBID 00016LNTH, off Road 91) in block M167 — LNTH; and an unnamed tributary to Maury Lake (WBID 01444MAHD, Road 20 at 23.75 km, crossed by Roads 291 and 295) in block I141 — Mahood watershed group (pp. 3, 21).
- **Type:** fish inventory (fish stream identification / stream classification by electrofishing and channel traverse under the FPC Fish Stream Identification Guidebook 1998)

## What was done
- Historical search in iMapBC, FISS, EcoCat and Watershed Atlas; reach breaks from TRIM; electrofishing with Smith Root 12B/15D units where water ≥5 °C and conductivity ≥30 µS (pp. 4–6). Barriers found in the field trigger electrofishing upstream to confirm absence.
- S258 trib to North Thompson (Sept 12): 336 m electrofished up- and downstream of the proposed crossing, 745 s, no fish; a further 404 m traversed downstream to the Weyerhaeuser operating-area boundary; 740 m total, no barriers found (p. 7).
- Cindy Lake outlet (Sept 13): lakeshore observed 27 min, no fish, >100 tadpoles; outlet traversed to the Road 122 crossing; flow too low to electrofish; DFO (Ron Hudema) has no stocking or depth data (p. 10).
- Bob Lake inlet (Sept 13): traversed 743.2 m upstream from the lake (p. 14).
- Efdee Lake inlet S4-2 (Sept 14): electrofished 151.6 m downstream from a deactivated road crossing, 577 s above and 70 s below a falls (p. 16).
- Maury Lake trib (Sept 14): traversed downstream from Road 20 into block I141; also checked at the Road 291 and Road 295 crossings; no electrofishing (water too shallow) (p. 19).

## Findings
(Table 1 p. 21)
- S258 trib to North Thompson: no fish captured but no barrier located; possible causes are a perched culvert downstream, a >20 % reach or no hydraulic connection through downstream wetlands in the Weyerhaeuser area; classified assumed fish, S3 (channel width 2.64 m) until a downstream barrier is documented (p. 7).
- Cindy Lake outlet: the perched culvert at Road 122 "may currently be a barrier to fish passage" (Figure 8 p. 12); habitat poor (no pools, no spawning gravel, 0.74 m channel); classified suspected/assumed fish, S4, pending a lake depth survey and netting (p. 10).
- Bob Lake inlet: a fry seen at 10U/693305/5745928 and six more fish upstream; no barriers over 743 m; confirmed fish from the lake to the point of traverse (10U/692928/5745777), assumed fish above; S4 (1.13 m channel). Bob Lake holds rainbow trout (FISS NT015) (p. 14).
- Efdee Lake inlet: a 1.38 m earthen falls 121.2 m below the deactivated crossing (10U/695646/5743547) is a barrier; no fish above or below in sampling; only a non-classified wetland upstream; confirmed non-fish above the falls, S6 (0.93 m) (p. 16). Efdee Lake itself holds rainbow and brook trout (FISS).
- Maury Lake trib: a 28 % gradient over 11.2 m at 377 m below Road 20 (10U/687133/5748652) is a barrier with no habitat above; culverts on Road 291 and Road 295 are both perched and "preventing the upstream movement of fish"; a fish was observed in the residual pool below the Road 291 culvert (about 10U/687057/5749722 as printed in Figure 17). Confirmed fish from Maury Lake up to Road 291, assumed fish up to the gradient barrier, non-fish above; reach 2 S6 (1.39 m) (pp. 19–20). Maury Lake holds rainbow trout (FISS).

## Recommendations and priorities
- S258: survey for barriers within the Weyerhaeuser operating area downstream; manage as S3 until a barrier is confirmed (Canfor said it would explore this) (p. 7).
- Cindy Lake: depth survey (>2 m, winter-kill risk) and netting to confirm presence/absence; manage the lake as amphibian habitat (p. 10).
- No crossing replacements are recommended or costed; the two perched culverts on Roads 291 and 295 and the Road 122 culvert are noted as barriers only.

## Useful for crossing work today
Not an FPCI report and not in PSCIS, but it supplies three field-observed perched culverts with fish below one of them: Road 291 and Road 295 on the Maury Lake tributary (Mahood group) — the very "downstream crossings on de-activated Roads 291 & 295" that the 2008 TFL 18 FPCI (24097, sites M-U20-3 to -12) could not reach and said must be assessed first — and Road 122 on the Cindy Lake outlet (Mann Creek system, LNTH). A planner should check whether those three crossings have PSCIS records and, if not, treat them as unassessed barrier candidates with confirmed rainbow trout downstream (Maury Lake) or an unsampled headwater lake above (Cindy Lake). The S258 result is also a pointer: a fishless North Thompson tributary with no barrier found in 740 m implies an unlocated barrier, possibly a culvert, downstream in the Weyerhaeuser area off the Caribou Mountain FSR.
