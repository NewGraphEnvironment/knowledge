# 24097 — Fish Passage Culvert Inspection (FPCI): Various Sites on TFL 18 (FIA 4972001); in the Lower North Thompson (LNT), Mahood (M) & Clearwater (CW) Watersheds — Final Report
- **Author / organisation:** Michele Trumbley, RPBio and Jenine Mylymok, Habitat Technologist; Trumbley Environmental Consulting Ltd. (TEC), Armstrong BC. Prepared for Canadian Forest Products Ltd. (Canfor), Vavenby Division.   **Year:** 2008 (draft Nov 14, 2008; final Nov 28, 2008)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** Forest Investment Account, project 4972001 (p. 4)
- **Region check:** in the North Thompson (LNTH) and adjacent groups. Sites are on TFL 18 in three watersheds as the report labels them: Lower North Thompson (LNT) — Mann Creek tributaries, Moira Lake, Hegar Lake, Calvin Lake, Cathy Lake, Moose Lake Creek, Beaver Lake, Frank Lake area; Mahood (M) — West/East Maury Lake, Ejas Lake, Sicily Lake, Double Lakes, Kitty Anne Lake, Twin Lakes, Boundary Line Lake, Herby Lake, Canimred Creek, Coldscaur Lake; and Clearwater (CW) — Grizzly Lakes, Reflector Lake, Brookfield Creek, Star Lake (Figure 1 p. 11; Figures 2–10 pp. 29–37). Only the LNT sites fall in the North Thompson watershed groups proper; Mahood and Clearwater sites are separate groups that drain to the Clearwater River.
- **Type:** fish passage assessment (Phase 1 culvert inspection under the MOE 2008 "Fish Passage Protocol for Culverted Sites" / "Field Assessment for Fish Passage Determination of Closed Bottom Structures, 2nd Ed., May 2008")

## What was done
- Ninety culvert crossings were pre-selected by Canfor Vavenby from local knowledge plus FSID, FHAP, FFHI and the Kamloops TSA 11 fish presence/absence mapping (Snowy River Resources 2006/2007) and TEC's own 2008 FSID reports (pp. 2, 6). Field dates are not stated beyond 2008.
- FPCI assessments were completed only on closed-bottom structures where fish presence was known or suspected. Scoring used the five-criterion Fish Barrier Scoring Table (embedment, outlet drop, slope, stream width ratio, length): score ≥20 = barrier, 15–19 = potential barrier, <15 = passable (Table 2 p. 8). Habitat value rated low/moderate/high (Table 1 p. 7). Habitat Gained Index (HGI) taken from TRIM 1:20,000 bluelines or field-verified FSID distances.
- Analysis rules (p. 9): barriers were not recommended for replacement where habitat was low/low–moderate and <1000 m of habitat lay upstream, or moderate/moderate–high and <500 m upstream.
- Cost-benefit (HGI km / $K) computed for every high-risk confirmed barrier using MOE FIA project summaries, ~$1130/m2 for arches and ~$1680/m2 for bridges (pp. 37–41). Implementation plan ranked with Canfor planning forester Dave Dobi (pp. 42–43).

## Findings
Status of the 90 sites (Executive Summary p. 2; the prose says 19 high-risk barriers with confirmed fish, the table says 20):
- High risk / full barrier, fish presence confirmed: 20 (Section 6.1.1, pp. 12–15). Highest scores: LNT-R6-12K trib to Mann Creek (39, 600 mm CMP, 41 cm drop, high habitat), M-WML-1 W. Maury Lake outlet (31, HGI 4,500 m), M-SR-1 trib to Canimred Creek (31), M-R20-EL trib 4 to Ejas Lake (31). Others scored 20–29: M-SR-3, M-BLL-1 (HGI 5,840 m), M-SL-2, M-U20-7, LNT-HL-3, LNT-R6-1, LNT-R6-2 (HGI 3,550 m, high habitat), LNT-R208-1, M-KAL-2 (replaced by Canfor fall 2008), LNT-R2-2, M-R295-7, M-TL-2, LNT-R91-1 (HGI 3,520 m), M-R295-2, LNT-ML-100, LNT-R10-CL1.
- High risk / full barrier, fish presence NOT confirmed: 8 (Section 6.1.2, p. 16): CW-R3-2 (36), CW-R3-3 (31), LNT-R7A-1 trib to Moose Lake Creek (31, HGI 2,630 m), M-R232-L3 (31), CW-R3-5 (26), CW-R3-6 (21), CW-R3-7 (21), LNT-R91-2 trib to Moira Lake (21, HGI 4,100 m).
- Moderate risk / potential barrier, fish confirmed: 4 (Section 6.2.1, p. 17): CW-R3-1 inlet to Grizzly Lakes (16, high habitat, HGI 3,480 m), M-KAL-1 Kitty Anne Lake outlet (16), M-DL-1 Sicily Lake outlet (16), LNT-R40A-1 trib to Moira Lake (scored passable at 6 as an open-bottom wooden box culvert, but it is collapsing and sill logs obstruct passage, so replacement recommended).
- Moderate not confirmed: 0. Low risk / passable: 0. Non-priority sites: 50 (pp. 19–27). 28 sites met the FIA minimum score of 20. Potential habitat gain if confirmed-fish barriers were removed: 38.1 km.
- Fish species are not named for most sites; fish presence at M-R1680-HL1 is inferred from fish moving downstream out of Herby Lake (p. 22), and M-TL-2 is recommended for replacement to maintain wild resident stocks in E. Twin Lake, which FISS does not show as stocked (p. 12).
- Many non-priority sites are scored barriers but have natural barriers (boulder cascades, falls, subsurface flow, >20 % gradients, non-classified drainages) within 25–730 m upstream or downstream, so no habitat would be gained: e.g. CW-R2-SLI (39), LNT-R2-FLI (24), LNT-R2-1 (26), LNT-R7A-2 (21), LNT-R91-3 (26), M-SL-1 (26), M-SR-2 (31; SRR 2005 also said do not restore), M-R1680-HL1 (26), CW-R3-8 (31), M-R297-1/2/3 (pp. 19–25).
- Several Road 20 sites in the upper Maury Lake system (M-U20-3, -5, -6, -8, -10, -12) were deferred because the downstream crossings on de-activated Roads 291 and 295 could not be accessed and should be assessed first (pp. 25–27). M-KAL-3/-4 and M-TL-3 need fish presence confirmed in headwater lakes 01670MAHD, 01672MAHD, 01847MAHD before FPCI (pp. 22, 25).
- Maintenance issues on priority sites (p. 28): road fill in the channel at outlets of LNT-R2-2 (11 3/4 km Road 2), M-SL-2 (22 3/4 km Road 20) and M-TL-2 (2 km Road 20).

## Recommendations and priorities
- Canfor priority ranking (Section 9.1, pp. 42–43), all high risk / confirmed fish:
  - Priority 1 (all LNT, Mann Creek system): LNT-R6-2 (10/686113/5737552, HGI 3.6 km, high habitat, OBS bridge $85K), LNT-R208-1 (10/686277/5738802, HGI 2.1 km, high, OBS bridge $84K), LNT-R6-12K (10/688131/5741507, HGI 1.0 km, bridge $85K or arch $78K), LNT-R6-1 Calvin Lake outlet (10/684813/5736123, HGI 0.6 km, bridge $86K or arch $83K).
  - Priority 2: M-SR-3 (arch $22K or embedded CMP $28K), M-SR-1 (bridge $62K), M-BLL-1 (bridge $84K or arch $55K), M-WML-1 (bridge $90K), LNT-R2-2 (bridge $85K), M-SL-2 (bridge $60K or arch $16K), M-TL-2 (bridge $84K).
- Best cost-benefit ratios among confirmed-fish barriers: M-SR-3 (0.091), M-SR-1 (0.073), LNT-ML-100 (0.073), M-R295-7 (0.073), M-BLL-1 (0.069/0.105 with arch), LNT-R91-1 (0.184 with arch) (pp. 38–40).
- For the 8 unconfirmed-fish barriers: confirm fish presence and habitat extent first; if fish absence is confirmed, install an overflow pipe or do nothing (LNT-R7A-1 arch $20K, LNT-R91-2 bridge $71K, CW-R3-6 $82K, CW-R3-3 $64K, CW-R3-5 $57K, CW-R3-2 $60K, CW-R3-7 $48K, M-R232-L3 $64K) (p. 41).
- Sites with TRIM-derived HGI may need a follow-up field visit to verify habitat; the ranking should be updated annually as fish presence data arrive (p. 42).

## Useful for crossing work today
This is the core 2008 FIA-era culvert dataset for TFL 18, with UTM coordinates, barrier scores and cost estimates for 90 crossings, and it was done under the MOE 2008 protocol, so the assessed sites are very likely already in PSCIS (check by coordinates; the report ids are Canfor road-based, not PSCIS ids). For the Lower North Thompson the four Priority 1 sites are all on Mann Creek tributaries off Roads 6 and 208, plus LNT-R2-2 and the two large-HGI Moira Lake tributaries (LNT-R91-1, LNT-R91-2); confirm whether any were replaced after 2008 before re-prioritising. The non-priority table is equally valuable: it documents natural barriers a short distance upstream of many scored barriers, which should stop those crossings being re-flagged by a habitat model. The Mahood and Clearwater sites belong to neighbouring watershed groups, and the deferred upper-Maury Road 291/295 crossings were later confirmed perched with fish below (see report 24281).
