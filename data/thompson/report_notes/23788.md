# 23788 — Letter: Inventory and Habitat Assessment of Thompson Brook, Kelowna, BC
- **Author / organisation:** Doug Wahl, Snowy River Resources Ltd. (Penticton), letter to Steve Matthews, RPBio, Senior Fisheries Biologist, Ministry of Environment and Lands, Penticton (p. 1).   **Year:** 2000 (letter dated May 8, 2000; field September 17, 1999)   **Funder / program (FIA, FRBC, DFO …):** completed at the request of the Ministry of Environment and Lands; no program stated.
- **Region check:** elsewhere — Okanagan. Thompson Brook is an urban creek in Kelowna (Lakeshore Drive, Fairview Golf Course, Armour Crescent) that only shares the name Thompson. Not in the North Thompson or South Thompson.
- **Type:** fish inventory (informal electrofishing population assessment with habitat notes).

## What was done
Continuous electrofishing (Smith-Root 12B POW, 350 s) over ~300 m from the confluence of Thompson Brook up to the section beside Fairview Golf Course on September 17, 1999 (p. 1). Only the first page of the letter is in the extracted text; it ends mid-sentence at the Armour Crescent crossing.

## Findings
Lower 50 m (below Lakeshore Drive): 3 longnose dace and 2 mottled sculpin, no salmonids; intact riparian vegetation but considerable silt/sediment loading and heavy algae (p. 1). Upstream of Lakeshore Drive the brook runs through a concrete flume; no fish captured there (p. 1). Anything beyond the Armour Crescent crossing is not in the extracted text.

## Recommendations and priorities
not stated (text truncated).

## Useful for crossing work today
Nothing for North Thompson crossing work; this is a Kelowna urban creek and should be excluded from the Thompson corpus as a false name match. The observations are not culvert-related and are unlikely to be in PSCIS.
