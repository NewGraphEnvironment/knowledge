- **[severity: bug]** research/nuseds_timing.md:41-42 — "the start before the survey on 15 of 20 Morice and 9 of 12 Nanika records with survey dates" (to 1991). The numerator (`start_before_survey`, data-raw/nuseds_dates_check.R:111) needs only a survey start `s0`; the denominator (`survey_dated`, :113) needs both `s0` and `s1`.   - **Rule:** code-check.md "One fact derived twice"

- **[severity: bug]** data-raw/nuseds_dates_read.R:64-75, 104 (and timing_nuseds.R:111) — `backwards` is tested on full dates whose century/year comes from `nuseds_date()`, but DFO often writes a January-March end with the record's own year (e.g. coho start 25-SEP-93, end 25-FEB-93; 01-SEP-98 to 01/10-JAN-98). Those records are flagged "end before they start" and dropped from bulk, peak and edge (`x$method <- x$present & !x$backwards`), though on the season-year days the windows are computed on they run forward. From the Parquet: 97 present records with a watershed code are backwards by date yet forward on the season year (CO 76, SK 8, CM 6, CH 4, PK 3), concentrated in a few populations (pop 51767: 18, 49825: 13, 46039: 12), so those populations' late ends, the case the season year exists for, are missing from their groups' bulk_end/peak. The headers' claim (nuseds_dates_check.R:19-20, "a coho end in January falls in the next year") holds only where DFO wrote the next year. Same root in the survey slips: of the 463 the research calls "a typing slip ... like it" (research/nuseds_timing.md:37-38), 98 are off-year and 365 reversed, 110 of the reversed ending January-April, i.e. winter surveys written with the record's year, not typos.
  - **Rule:** code-check.md "A proxy is not the property"
