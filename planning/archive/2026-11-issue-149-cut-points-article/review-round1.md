## Findings

- **[severity: bug]** vignettes/_cut_points.Rmd:117-118, 336-337, 340 — The prose says the close-call verdict holds for a slope **limit**, but the guard tests one exact **rule**. Rendered sentences: "on the minimal network's records the 13 % limit fits measurably worse"; "take the limit to 13 %, which is not a close call on the minimal network's records"; "The Atlas line's slopes choose 15 %, a close call on neither network's records". `in_reg()` (lines 69-74, guards at 78-81) checks only whether the exact pick, e.g. `mad>=0.75|g<=0.13|Q3@D1000`, is in the other table's region. Measured on the bundle's tune tables: 13 rules with `g<=0.13` are close calls on the minimal network's records, one of them with the same `mad>=0.75` floor. 23 rules with `g<=0.15` are close calls on the full network's records, and 11 on the minimal network's. So the 13 % limit is a close call on the minimal records, and 15 % is a close call on both. Only the exact rules are outside. research/minimal_network_tuning.md:181-182 states the same result correctly, by rule ("the published 13 % lies in the Atlas line's region"). The sentences need to name the rule (limit together with floor and allowance), or the guard needs to test every rule with that `grad_max`.
  - **Rule:** code-check.md "A proxy is not the property" ; code-check.md "A guard's scope, escape hatches, and remedies"

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/_cut_points.Rmd:210-216, 262-264 (and 229-231 vs 254-258) — Several numbers described as the minimal network's are computed from `parent_gradient`, the full segment's minimal parent. The prose says "against … on the minimal network's segments", "At 407 sites the two networks put the slope on opposite sides", and "whose full-network slope and minimal-network slope sit on opposite sides". The minimal network's own reading at a site is `minimal_gradient`, and at 14 of the 7,823 population sites the two differ, by up to 0.124. At those sites the site sits 1-4.5 m below a minimal-network cut (`minimal_offset_m`), so the visit falls on the minimal segment below its full segment's parent. Read on `minimal_gradient`, 408 sites change side, not 407. The examples are chosen on `parent_gradient` (`e$site$crosses`, line 230), but the strip figure draws the minimal segment, whose slope the guard holds to `minimal_gradient` (lines 256-258). Nothing requires the two to agree for an example. They agree for today's three, but a rebuilt bundle could draw an example whose two boxes sit on the same side of the limit, under a caption saying they do not. research/minimal_network_tuning.md:335 ("every visit's parent is the minimal segment it sits on") is contradicted by these 14 sites. One fix is to say "minimal parent" wherever `parent_gradient` is read. Another is to add `minimal_gradient` to the example criterion and guard.
  - **Rule:** code-check.md "One fact derived twice" ; code-check.md "A proxy is not the property"
