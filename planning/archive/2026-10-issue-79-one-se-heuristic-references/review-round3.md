## The mechanism behind rounds 1 and 2

All six earlier defects share one assumption: **a string means to its next consumer what it means to its author.**
Each value was right as written and checked where it was produced, and wrong under the grammar or audience of
whatever read it next:

| round | value | next consumer | what that consumer does with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a home path and credential-store wording in planning notes | the public repo | publishes it |
| 1 | a Zotero title used as a `sub()` replacement | R's replacement grammar | strips backslashes |
| 2 | the round-1 review quoting what round 1 removed | the public repo and git history | publishes the strings again, from a new file |
| 2 | a raw `³` in an R string | `R CMD check`'s ASCII rule | warns |
| 2 | an empty ledger note read as `NA` | `nzchar()` | treats NA as filled |
| 2+ | the bib's own path guard written with a literal home-dir prefix | the public repo | the guard leaks what it guards against |

The fixes also tend to land at one instance and miss its twin. That is "A fix lands in one of two callers that share a
harness", and finding 1 is the case.

## Findings

- **[fragile] tests/testthat/test-vignette-data.R:84**: `expect_true(all(nzchar(unlist(x$meta$versions))))` is the
  `nzchar(NA)` defect that round 2 fixed in `data-raw/vignette_chinook.R:218`. The fix went into the script and not
  into this test, which pins the bundle the article and `vignette_bib.R` read. A bundle carrying an NA version passes
  it (`all(nzchar(unlist(list(a = "1", b = NA_character_))))` is `TRUE`). It does not fire today: all six values are
  filled. Add `expect_false(anyNA(unlist(x$meta$versions)))`, or use the script's `!is.na(...) & nzchar(...)`.

- **[bug] vignettes/chinook_rearing.Rmd:270-272, with data-raw/vignette_chinook.R:225-230 (and the header comment at
  :28)**: the sentence "A stretch through a wetland of 1 ha or more is rearing under both rules whatever its size,
  slope or distance from a river (link's wetland rule): about 10,200 km" describes a set the code does not count, and
  states a property the rule does not have.
  - **The set.** `out$wetland` counts rearing on edge types 1050 and 1150 only. link's wetland branch is "edge
    1000/1100/1050/1150 in a wetland ≥ 1 ha" (`research/chinook_rearing_tuned.md`, "What `default` does"). On the
    shipped BULK bundle, 77 km of `default`'s pre-connectivity rearing sit on edge-1000/1100 lines inside wetlands of
    1 ha or more that fail the stream test (gradient over 5.49 % or width under 1.5 m). Only the wetland branch makes
    those rearing, and the figure leaves them out. For comparison, the edge-1050/1150 lines the figure does count
    carry 259 km of pre-connectivity rearing in BULK.
  - **"Is rearing ... whatever its distance".** The wetland branch is followed by the connectivity pass. On BULK's
    edge-1050/1150 lines in wetlands of 1 ha or more, all 259 km pass the habitat test (`pre_tuned`), and 179 km
    remain rearing under the tuned rule. The other 80 km (31 %) are dropped because they do not connect to spawning.
  - **The fix.** Count the branch the sentence names, or narrow the sentence to what is counted. Either way, say that
    a wetland stretch must still connect to spawning. The `stopifnot(abs(wl$km_tuned - 10200) < 200, ...)` premise
    will need re-pinning if the count changes.

- **[fragile] R/compare_map.R:284**: `paste0(wa, ", ", signif(seg$water_ha, 3), " ha")` turns the area into text
  through `as.character()`, which writes `1e+05`, `2e+05` and `1e-04` for areas that round to those values. The comment
  at :261 states the rule ("per value, never format()"), and `sig2` exists for it, but this field bypasses both. It
  does not fire on BULK (0.029 to 654 ha). `knw_compare_mapgl()` is exported and takes any group, though, and
  province-wide lakes reach 10^5 ha. `trimws(formatC(signif(v, 3), digits = 3, format = "fg"))` gives `100000`,
  `0.0001` and `654`.
