## Verdicts checked against each row's own reading

- `prior_sd` (line 320) is marked **aligned** although the row lists four departures. That is defensible, because the
  row is about the prior's purpose and scale and the one claimed defect sits in its own row. After F1 and F4 the
  departures are family and link only, so **aligned** stands more cleanly.
- `fallback_no_prior` (369): **should change** follows from the row's reading, but the reading is wrong (F1).
- `unseen_reference` (370): **should change** does not follow once F2(c) is read. The reference is the prior mean.
- `gradient_col` (316) and `honest_nesting` (374): **should change** each comes with a concrete change (#70; nest, or describe).
- No row describes a defect and then says aligned. No **should change** lacks a concrete change. ISSUE_A's
  "first measurement" (fallback count) is concrete but will read 0 (F1).
