## Findings

- **[wrong-claim]** `data/literature_evidence.csv` data rows 1082, 1083, 1084, 1085, 1088, 1091 (the shared `M5` sentence in `planning/active/draft_rows.py`), and `planning/active/findings.md` "The 2005 report and the 2007 paper share their fitted curves", first sentence: "The report's growth curves and 60-d survival curve use the third WCT and the second RB experiment (p. 9)". This is right for the growth curves and wrong for the survival curve. Report Fig. 5 (p. 27), whose points and coefficients are identical to 2007 Fig. 1, pools every experiment:
  - It plots 16 WCT test temperatures (8–30 °C; p. 11 lists them too), but each experiment had 12 (p. 8: "Three replicates were tested for each of the 12 testing temperatures").
  - It has nine WCT tanks at 20 °C (about 92, 94 and 96%, plus 19, 9, 6, 4, 2 and 0%) and six at 22 °C.
  - It has six RB tanks at 24 °C (96–98% and 42–54%). These are the two RB experiments that p. 15 contrasts ("decreased survival (47%) ... improved survival (97%)").
  - The report's own "intermediate at 20 °C (36%)" (p. 11) is the paper's 35.7% (p. 1115), which is the mean over all three WCT experiments: (2 × 51.9 + 3.3)/3 = 35.7, from the small-fish and large-fish means on p. 1117. The paper's 12.5% at 22 °C is likewise the mean of the six Fig. 5 tanks, (57 + 10 + 6 + 2 + 0 + 0)/6.
  - p. 9's "only the results from the third experiment are reported here" holds only for growth. Figs. 1–2 have three tanks per temperature, and 2007 p. 1115's omitted RB 12 °C replicate shows as two points in Fig. 2.
  - The sentence was carried over from p. 9's prose after the LD50 figures had been checked; the survival figure, whose caption names no experiments, was never read for it.
- **[wrong-claim]** Data rows 1082–1085 (the same `M5` sentence): "satiation ration (p. 8)". These rows' values come from the survival curve or the LD50 regressions. Both pool WCT experiments 1–2 and RB experiment 1, and p. 9 says those were fed "a fixed ration ... later determined to be less than satiation consumption at some test temperatures". p. 8 describes the protocol, not the pooled data. The sentence is right on rows 1088 and 1091, whose growth data come from the satiation-fed experiments (p. 9).
- **[wrong-claim]** `planning/active/findings.md`, same section: "Only some survival percentages are pooled in the paper (RB at 24 °C: 72.8%, between the report's two experiments)." The paper's survival percentages and its Fig. 1 are all pooled, and so are the report's (Fig. 5, and the 36% on p. 11), as the first finding shows. 72.8% is the two-experiment mean, (97.3 + 48.3)/2. Contrasting it with the report is wrong, because the report pools the same tanks.

The same pooled survival data, though, are described in report p. 9 as partly from sub-satiation fixed rations. The row is true to its page, but the two sources disagree about these data and no note records it. A note on 1071/1072 would stop a reader from treating the 19.6 and 24.3 °C UUILTs as satiation-only.

## Mechanism

The mechanism is the one named, but narrower. A methods sentence was taken from the prose and applied to every product, then corrected only where a figure contradicted it out loud. p. 9 says only the third WCT and second RB experiments are reported. Round 3 found that the LD50 figures, whose captions name "each of three experiments", break that, so the note was rewritten as "growth and survival curves use the third, LD50 regressions pool all". That is a reconciliation reasoned from the two captions that were read. Fig. 5's caption names no experiments, so its point count (16 temperatures, 9 tanks at 20 °C) was never checked against p. 8's 12 temperatures × 3 replicates. The same reasoning produced the second sentence of the pair: "satiation ration (p. 8)" was copied onto rows whose data include the sub-satiation experiments, and findings.md's "only some survival percentages are pooled in the paper". Each fix restated a neighbour ("the curves" as a single class) instead of checking each figure's data.

This enumeration ends it because it checked every claim that relates the two sources or names the data a value comes from. Each one was checked against figure point counts and the report's arithmetic (36% = 35.7% = the three-experiment mean; 72.8% = the two-experiment mean; 12.5% = the six-tank mean), not against captions. What remains wrong is the two `M5` sentences, on six rows (1082, 1083, 1084, 1085, 1088, 1091), and the two sentences in findings.md. A correct M5 would read roughly: "The growth curves use the third WCT and second RB experiment (p. 9; Figs. 1–2, three tanks per temperature); the 60-d survival curve (Fig. 5) and the LD50 regressions (Figs. 6, 8) pool all three WCT and both RB experiments, which include the fixed sub-satiation rations of the earlier experiments (p. 9)." The satiation clause would then appear only on the growth rows.
