- **[severity: bug]** research/chinook_stream_size.md:69-71 (and the `regulated` column of the table at 73-83). The text says the regulated column is the "share of the class's detections". 0295 computes `regulated_share = mean(regulated)` over the class's **visits** (0295:195; the 0295 header calls it "share of visits"). The two differ a lot. For example, 0.1–0.28 m³/s shows 2 %, but 16 of its 57 CH detections (28 %) are on regulated reaches: Nechako/Cheslatta side channels. For 30–100 the visit share is 66 % and the detection share 83 %; for ≥ 100 it is 81 % and 92 %. As written, a reader concludes that the 0.1–0.28 detections are free of regulation, which is false. Fix one of two ways: relabel the column as a share of visits, or add the detection share.
- **[severity: bug]** research/chinook_stream_size.md:24. "Below 0.5 m³/s, CH are caught at a third to a seventh of the average rate." The listed values are 0.14, 0.47 and 0.61 (data/ch_mad_use.csv mad_pick/ch_caught). That is a seventh up to about three-fifths, not a third; two of the three classes are above a third.
- **[severity: bug]** research/chinook_stream_size.md:111-112. "agree on the class for only 53–67 % of visits between 0.1 and 1 m³/s, against 90–99 % elsewhere." The log's agreement table (20261015_ch_mad_use.txt, "source agreement on the class") gives the other classes as <0.1 0.941, 1-3 0.754, 3-10 0.902, 10-30 0.832, 30-100 0.851 and >=100 0.990. So "elsewhere" is 75–99 %, and 1–3 m³/s (75 %) is closer to the weak band than to 90 %.
- **[severity: fragile]** research/chinook_stream_size.md:108-109. "from 0.5 m³/s it is above 1, except 1–3 m³/s with barriers filtered (0.84)". sens_fwapg_old_mad ≥ 100 reads rel_rate 0 (9 visits, 0 detections, underpowered). The quantifier is false for that cell. It is minor, but the sentence lists fwapg's old MAD among the analyses it covers.
- **[severity: fragile]** research/chinook_stream_size.md:33. "Within 500 m downstream of a ≥ 10 m³/s segment". The walk measures from the visit **down** to the large segment, so the visit lies upstream of the large segment, i.e. the large river is within 500 m downstream of the visit. Read literally, the sentence puts the visit downstream of the large river, which is the reverse of what was measured. Line 92's "downstream distance to MAD ≥ 10" has it right.
