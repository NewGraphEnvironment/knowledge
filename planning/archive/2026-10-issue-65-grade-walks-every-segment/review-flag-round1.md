## Findings

- **[false-claim]** `planning/archive/2026-10-issue-65-grade-walks-every-segment/README.md:55`: "59–94 % of visit sites end on a double-line river". The script's denominator is the sites whose walk found a river (`f`), not all visit sites. The research file keeps that qualifier ("Of the curves' visit sites whose walk found a river"); the README drops it. Over all sites the range is about 53–93 %. ST is 0.87 × 614 / 1001 ≈ 53 %, CO 0.74 × 2854 / 3530 ≈ 60 % and CH ≈ 58 %, so the low end is ST, not BT spawning. Restore "of the sites whose walk found a river".

- **[false-claim]** `research/habitat_thresholds_tuned.md:225-226`: "Off the stream branch is 24–36 % of each key's graded km **but** 21–79 % of its km at 0.8 or more". The "but" asserts that off-branch km is over-represented at 0.8 or more. It is for five keys: CH 34→79, ST 30→70, CO 36→66, BT_rear 24→34, and WCT only just (34→35). For three it is under-represented: BT_spawn 24→21, GR 32→29 and RB 30→24 (`check_off_branch.txt`, off_pct against hi_off_pct). This is an every-key sentence carrying the pattern of some keys, the error class the archive README's lesson names. Either drop the contrast or name the keys it holds for.

- **[false-claim]** `research/habitat_thresholds_tuned.md:223-225`: "A main-flow line through a lake or wetland on a river (edge type 1200, four fifths of them in lakes) reads 0 m from it the same way (CH: 20 % of graded edge-1200 km grades 0.8 or more)". The parenthetical makes the subject all of edge type 1200, and "the same way" ties it to river polygons, which read 0 m on 48–79 % of their km. For CH, only 18 % of graded edge-1200 km has `dist_m` ≤ 1 m: I measured 18 % over 14,788 km from `CH_rear_grades.csv`, and round 2 of the branch review also measured 18 %. The 20 % at 0.8+ is offered as support, but it is measured over all edge-1200 km, not over the subset "on a river". Either state the within-1 m share for edge 1200 (18 % for CH) or confine the claim to the lines it measures.

- **[false-claim / provenance]** `research/habitat_thresholds_tuned.md:3-17, 223-237`: the new figures have no producer in the file. These are the off-branch shares, the edge-1200 figures, the connector medians and percentiles, and 22 of 3,862. The header's "Produced by" list names only `0300`–`0330`. The check scripts are named only in the archive README. The "four fifths of them in lakes" figure has no producer anywhere: neither check script computes lake against wetland, and I had to measure it (84 % for CH, by km). It is also stated without a key, as if it held across species. Under the repo's research convention ("Never a number without its producer"), name `planning/archive/2026-10-issue-65-grade-walks-every-segment/check_off_branch.R` and `check_connector_share.R` in the header, and either add the lake share to `check_off_branch.R` or attribute it to CH.

- **[fragile]** `check_connector_share.R` (the `dl` subset): the median, the 90th percentile, the share of distance and `flips_exc` are computed over `dl`. That is the sites ending on edge 1250 whose river's `wscode_ltree` is an ancestor of the site's (`rws @> vws`). Sites ending on a 1250 that fail the ancestry test, or that have a NULL `wscode_ltree`, get `conn_m = NA` and drop out silently. The script never prints `nrow(dl)` beside the number of 1250-ending sites. So "there the connector adds a median of 14–46 m" and "22 of the 3,862 CH sites" rest on an unreported subset. The 22 is a lower bound only if every dropped site is excluded correctly. Print `n_double_line` and `n_dl` per key before the text cites these as covering the double-line endings.
