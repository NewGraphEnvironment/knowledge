# knowledge: Tests Fish Habitat Definitions Against What is Known About Where Fish Live, and Tunes Them from the Fish Records

Gathers what is known about where fish live in British Columbia's
streams (life history and habitat values from the cited literature with
their quotes, past reports, intrinsic-potential models, life-history
timing and the provincial fish sampling records) and tunes from the
records the gradient and stream-size thresholds of the fish habitat
definitions the link habitat models use: it places sampling visits on
link's stream segments, scores candidate habitat definitions on held-out
watershed groups, grades segments by expected use, and tabulates, maps
and compares the tuned habitat definitions with link's defaults. It also
pulls PSCIS crossing layers and past-report tables for a group and
checks stream names before a search. Under active development.
