# research

What is known, revised in place; `git log --follow` is each file's dated history.
Naming: `<topic>.md`, from 2026-09-26.

| file | covers |
|---|---|
| `database_builds.md` | what a retune reads from the database and where it can run: the round trip as a fixed cost per query and what batching 500 walks per query bought (host and remote link), the walk cache, the comparison with a key's groups in parallel, the build check at connect, and what a second database needs (#87) |
| `fdis_export.md` | the province-supplied FDIS electrofishing export: join structure, units, defects, surveys loaded under several collection ids and the copy kept (#35), how a visit is placed on its stream with fresh's snap and the 1:50k code and the snap limits by measurement (#91), and what it adds over the public FISS layers |
| `run_archive.md` | keeping a tuning run (#119): what the archive holds and where (grade tables compressed, records, curves, segments; the evidence once per build), the tracked record the bundles pin instead of the tables, the index of version ids, overwrite with versioning and expiry by prefix, and the cost |
| `zotero_database.md` | reading Zotero's local database while it runs (#127): Zotero 10's WAL mode, why a copy of the main file alone or an `immutable=1` read is silently stale, what a guarded copy of the file and its `-wal` needs (each piece measured by removing it), and what it cannot see |
| `habitat_literature.md` | what the literature says about CH and BT habitat thresholds: the origin of each modelled value, what has no source, BT reach-scale evidence, and how the evidence table's quotes are verified |
| `ip_models.md` | the Pacific Northwest intrinsic-potential models: what each thresholds on, how each sized streams (gauged, modelled or surrogate flow, from what inputs), validation, and what that means for width vs MAD in BC |
| `habitat_weights.md` | what BT rearing use by gradient, elevation, channel size and effort means: effort-corrected detection on FDIS visits placed on link's segments, the any-fish vs BT-given-fish split, a position per driver, and the candidate weight per gradient class |
| `bt_spawning.md` | what fish records and the literature say about BT spawning gradient and channel size: spawner evidence on link's segments (within-WSG null, size split), the literature's observed spawning gradients (0.6–4 %, no limit given), the descriptive transit table, and what redd layers would need |
| `stream_temperature.md` | the Nechako and Skeena stream-temperature network models (air2stream in Stan): structure, fitted values, what they can and cannot do (gap-fill yes, open-loop; no unmonitored segments; no out-of-sample validation), re-run state, and what temperature data and evidence NGE holds. |
| `thermal_requirements.md` | cited temperature limits and degree-day requirements for BT, WCT, CH, CO and ST by life stage, each with its metric (#38, #40), and first-winter size thresholds (#47): what is known, what has no source (no BC growing-season degree-day requirement, after a search beyond the collection), and which values wet#40 and link#21 should use |
| `modelled_discharge.md` | which modelled flow to use: wet's published monthly and annual flow per FWA segment (routed PCIC and wet's open water balance, `pick`, scores at the calibration gauges, Chinook-window flows, normal years only, not yet read by link); and what it is built from: fwapg's annual MAD from PCIC's gridded VIC-GL runoff (Fraser, Peace, Columbia), PCIC's Raven-routed hydromosaic flows, and a gauge check at Houston (drought low flows overstated) |
| `flow_requirements.md` | cited flow needs (`instream_flow`), redd and passage depths and spawning and rearing velocities for CH, CO, SK, ST, BT, GR and WCT by life stage (#44): Ptolemy's BC standards as the generic floor, species needs for CH and ST, thin for BT, none for GR and WCT; how a need compares with #42's monthly flows, and what could not be obtained |
| `chinook_stream_size.md` | where juvenile CH are caught by stream size (#52), from FDIS visits in link's CH range: effort-corrected CH detection by wet's MAD class (relative rate 0.14–0.61 under 0.5 m³/s), small streams with a ≥ 10 m³/s segment within 500 m downstream (rate ratio 26.7 against those further away), regulated rivers in > 30 m³/s (the Bridge below Terzaghi at 30–100, one Nechako survey at ≥ 100), and what it means for link's CH rearing bounds |
| `habitat_thresholds_tuned.md` | the one tuning run across species and stages on link's province-wide `default` run (#57, retuned on the rebuilt evidence by #73): the method (0297's held-out spec for every species, the grid's fit and prior, the joint curve, the 0–1 grade and its gate), the table of recommended rules and regions (`default` outside the one-SE region for every fitted key but grayling), the grade summary per key (none for CO, ST and WCT), what each rule changes on link's segments under the through-wetland door, and the picks at 1.96 SE (#79) |
| `bull_trout_tuned.md` | BT rearing (MAD ≥ 0.35 m³/s, ≤ 25 %: the grid's best rule, its limit in a flat stretch to the ladder's top) and spawning (width ≥ 2 m, ≤ 13 %, weak; no grade: its curve is behind no rule on its own visits) rules on the province-wide run (#73), superseding #28's class weight |
| `coho_rearing_tuned.md` | CO rearing: MAD ≥ 0.03 m³/s, gradient ≤ 4 %; coho rear in the smallest streams (the pick unchanged by #73's retune); no grade, its curve behind no rule on held-out groups; beside link's `default` the door on through-wetland lines costs 2.7 points more than the free pass and the checks cannot tell them apart |
| `grayling_rearing_tuned.md` | GR rearing: `default` (width ≥ 1.5 m, ≤ 3.49 %) is inside the one-SE region and is the pick, confirmed weakly (0.9 SE behind the best); the curve beats every cut, so it is graded |
| `rainbow_rearing_tuned.md` | RB rearing: width ≥ 3 m, ≤ 30 %, the top of the ladder (the data say a limit near `default`'s 8.49 % costs, not where the limit is); the largest population; a width rule grades measured streams only |
| `steelhead_rearing_tuned.md` | ST rearing: MAD ≥ 1 m³/s, ≤ 8.49 %, on 82 detections in 11 groups; no grade, the curve behind no rule on held-out groups even with the prior |
| `westslope_cutthroat_rearing_tuned.md` | WCT rearing on six groups with both outcomes: underpowered; MAD ≥ 0.03 m³/s, ≤ 30 %, an underpowered lean; no grade |
| `chinook_rearing_tuned.md` | the tuned CH rearing rule (#54's 55-group build, rescored province-wide by #57 and retuned on the rebuilt evidence by #73 as `CH_rear_v1`): the life-history prior, the evidence, the recommended rule (gradient ≤ 13 %, and either MAD ≥ 0.75 m³/s or a ≥ 3 m³/s river within 1 km; `default` outside the region in every reading), the pick from link's flow start (#80), what each check says, the ladder to 30 % (#67, #68: flat from 11 to 30 %), the intercept fit, the grade, the rule beside link's `default` under three structures (#81), and #54's record of the method and sensitivities |
