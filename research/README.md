| `flow_requirements.md` | cited flow needs (`instream_flow`), redd and passage depths and spawning and rearing velocities for CH, CO, SK, ST, BT, GR and WCT by life stage (#44): Ptolemy's BC standards as the generic floor, species needs for CH and ST, thin for BT, none for GR and WCT; how a need compares with #42's monthly flows, and what could not be obtained |
| `chinook_stream_size.md` | where juvenile CH are caught by stream size (#52), from FDIS visits in link's CH range: effort-corrected CH detection by wet's MAD class (relative rate 0.14–0.61 under 0.5 m³/s), small streams with a ≥ 10 m³/s segment within 500 m downstream (rate ratio 26.7 against those further away), regulated rivers in > 30 m³/s (the Bridge below Terzaghi at 30–100, one Nechako survey at ≥ 100), and what it means for link's CH rearing bounds |
| `chinook_rearing_tuned.md` | a tuned CH rearing rule (#54): 2,795 size, gradient and near-a-river rules scored on held-out WSGs from FDIS visits on link's stream branch, the recommended gradient ≤ 7 % and either MAD ≥ 0.75 m³/s or a ≥ 3 m³/s river within 1 km (MAD ≥ 0.5 at 7–9 % is in every reading's region) (`default` and its MAD bounds outside the region), the range of each value, curves with no bend, where the flow source moves it, and the rule beside `default` on link's segments (−18 % CH rearing km), with bcfishobs and FDIS checks |
